Loaded core + thrown core, if they differ, writes a cataclysm. Swappable freely.

**plasma + acid**

Target shakes. Arcs crawl the body. Acid bubbles form on limbs, rupture from the arcs, spray out. Fade: blue-green puddle throwing plasma sparks.

**plasma + void**

Arcs slam in. Outline blue → white. Jitter. Rupture into shards that freeze mid-air. A tear drinks them. Shimmering crater remains.

**acid + void**

Acid eats chunks. Void shows through the holes. Puddles lift and warp, then ingest. A shadow-hollow flickers into purple shards.

**plasma**

Throw: pulse blue → white → navy, arcs, trail. Burst: implode then erupt, sphere, shards. Fade: lingering sparks, impact disk.

**acid**

Throw: drips that sizzle, vapor trail. Burst: mist and sticking liquid, secondary arcing drops. Fade: bubbling puddles, hiss.

**void**

Throw: hum, shimmer in/out. Burst: pull inward, then black→purple shockwave. Fade: shifting crater, hovering singularity.
